Product: women's crinkle cotton pajamas, one size (45-80 kg), Saudi Arabia and Gulf, SAR 99, COD and bank transfer.

## Market (to verify with real data, not claims)
- Competitors to review by hand: local Instagram-first pajama brands and marketplace sellers (Noon, Amazon.sa, Shein). Check their price, fabric claim, delivery time and return terms, and record them here with the date.

## Strategy
1. Get indexed (Google Search Console, Bing already verified) and rank on long-tail Arabic terms: بيجامة قطن نسائي السعودية, بيجامة كوريشة, هدية بيجامة.
2. Convert with trust: real photos, real customer reviews after delivery, clear returns.
3. Grow by content on the site (blog, city pages, gift guide), not paid ads.
4. Measure: visits, add-to-cart, checkout drop-off, orders (dashboard analytics).
